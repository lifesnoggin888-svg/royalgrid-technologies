# Company Thesis

**Type: FACT (positioning as adopted) / TARGET (product maturity)**

RoyalGrid Technologies is building the opportunity-access infrastructure for African
businesses. Its platform continuously discovers and verifies funding, procurement,
accelerator, export, supplier, and development-finance opportunities, matches them against a
company's eligibility, and converts fragmented opportunity information into an
evidence-backed path from discovery to application readiness.

## Why this problem

African businesses do not typically miss opportunities because they are unqualified. They
miss them because:

1. Opportunities are published across thousands of disconnected portals, agencies, and
   institutions, each with its own format and cadence.
2. There is no reliable way to verify whether a listing is current, expired, or fabricated.
3. Eligibility requirements are written in dense, jurisdiction-specific language that takes
   real effort to interpret against a specific company.
4. Even qualified businesses miss deadlines because required documents or certifications are
   identified too late to prepare.

## What RoyalGrid is, and is not

RoyalGrid is infrastructure — a discovery, verification, matching, and readiness pipeline —
not a static directory or a single grant-finder tool. The flagship product, OpportunityGrid,
is described in [docs/product/OPPORTUNITYGRID.md](../product/OPPORTUNITYGRID.md).

RoyalGrid does not claim to be a funding source, a guarantee of eligibility, or a replacement
for the issuing institution's own decision process. Its role is discovery, verification, and
readiness — not underwriting or approval.
