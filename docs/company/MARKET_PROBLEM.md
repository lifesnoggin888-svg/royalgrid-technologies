# Market Problem

**Type: FACT (problem description) / ASSUMPTION (sizing) — no numerical market claims are
published until they are sourced.**

## The fragmentation problem

Opportunities relevant to African businesses — grants, accelerators, procurement/tenders,
corporate supplier programs, export initiatives, development finance, innovation challenges,
and SME support programs — are published by a large and disconnected set of issuers:
government agencies, multilateral development institutions, corporates, accelerators, and
non-profits. There is no single, verified, current index of these opportunities for African
businesses today.

## Why existing approaches fall short

- **Manual search** does not scale past a handful of known sources and misses anything not
  actively searched for.
- **Static directories** go stale quickly; deadlines and requirements change without the
  directory being updated.
- **Generic "grant finder" tools** typically return unverified or U.S./Europe-centric results
  and do not model African registration types, jurisdictions, or eligibility criteria.
- **Word of mouth / social media** carries no source, issuer, or verification and is a common
  vector for outdated or fraudulent listings.

## What we will not claim

This document intentionally does not include total addressable market figures, opportunity
volume estimates, or business population counts. Those numbers will be added only once
sourced from citable, dated data (e.g., national statistics agencies, development finance
institution reporting) — see [docs/investor/INVESTMENT_THESIS.md](../investor/INVESTMENT_THESIS.md)
for how market sizing will be handled once available.
