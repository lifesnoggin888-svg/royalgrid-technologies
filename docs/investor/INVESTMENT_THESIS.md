# Investment Thesis

**Type: TARGET / ASSUMPTION where noted. This is not a solicitation and contains no
committed terms.**

## Problem (FACT)

African businesses lose access to funding, procurement, and growth opportunities not for
lack of qualification but because discovery, verification, eligibility, and application
readiness are fragmented across thousands of disconnected sources. See
[../company/MARKET_PROBLEM.md](../company/MARKET_PROBLEM.md).

## Solution (CURRENT CAPABILITY / TARGET)

RoyalGrid Technologies' OpportunityGrid platform (see
[../product/OPPORTUNITYGRID.md](../product/OPPORTUNITYGRID.md)) is being built on an
evidence-bound retrieval foundation, with a discover-verify-match-qualify-prepare-track
pipeline architected for African-first, multi-country operation.

## Business model (TARGET — modeled, not yet monetized)

Three revenue paths are being modeled, not currently generating revenue:

1. **SME subscription** — opportunity discovery, matching, readiness, and tracking.
2. **Professional / business-development tier** — multiple entities, collaboration,
   document readiness, and pipelines.
3. **Institutional licensing / API** — banks, accelerators, SME networks, chambers, DFIs,
   and supplier ecosystems.

Success-fee economics are explicitly not the primary model at this stage.

## Why now (ASSUMPTION, stated as such)

The underlying retrieval and evidence-bound reasoning technology already exists as a working
prototype (`grant-scout`), reducing technical risk for the Nigeria-first build compared to
starting from zero. This is an assumption about execution risk, not a claim of product-market
fit, which has not yet been tested with OpportunityGrid specifically.
